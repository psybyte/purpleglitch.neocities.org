Minority Report Visual Style Guide (for Web Use)

General Aesthetic - Cinematic near-future sci‑fi inspired by the film
Minority Report (2002). - Emphasis on a cold, technological, atmospheric
mood.

Visual Characteristics - Strong bloom and halation on highlights. -
Overexposed backlight creating glowing silhouettes. - Soft diffusion:
slightly blurred edges and gentle glow. - Mild bleach-bypass look: low
saturation, deep blacks, gritty texture.

Color Palette - Dominant hues: cold blues, cyan tones, metallic greys. -
Very low saturation; nearly monochromatic. - Bright whites with soft
glowing edges.

Atmosphere - Fog, mist, light rain, and environmental haze. - Futuristic
cityscapes with silhouettes fading into distance. - Wet reflective
surfaces enhancing bloom.

Composition - Shallow depth of field: sharp subjects, blurred
backgrounds. - Dynamic framing and light motion blur for energy. -
Tension-driven tone: surveillance, pursuit, anticipation.

Summary (Short Version) “Futuristic cinematic style with intense bloom,
cold cyan color grading, soft diffusion, atmospheric fog, and a
bleach-bypass look—reflecting the aesthetic of Minority Report.”
