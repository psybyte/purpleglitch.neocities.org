Minority Report Visual Style Guide (for Web Use)

General Aesthetic - Cinematic near-future sci‑fi inspired by the film
Minority Report (2002). - Emphasis on a cold, technological, atmospheric
mood. - Layered digital decay and neon-infused futurism.

Visual Characteristics - Strong bloom and halation on highlights. -
Overexposed backlight creating glowing silhouettes. - Soft diffusion:
slightly blurred edges and gentle glow. - Mild bleach-bypass look: low
saturation, deep blacks, gritty texture. - Chromatic aberration on 
high-contrast edges for digital distortion. - Scanlines and interlace 
patterns suggesting holographic/digital surfaces. - Lens flares and 
geometric light refractions from tech elements.

Color Palette - Dominant hues: cold blues, cyan tones, metallic greys. -
Very low saturation; nearly monochromatic. - Bright whites with soft
glowing edges. - Accent neon streaks: electric cyan, vaporwave magenta,
and phosphorescent green for digital UI elements. - Desaturated warm
tones (beige, burnt sienna) for contrast against cool palette.

Atmosphere - Fog, mist, light rain, and environmental haze. - Futuristic
cityscapes with silhouettes fading into distance. - Wet reflective
surfaces enhancing bloom. - Particle systems: dust motes, rain droplets,
digital artifacts drifting through space. - Film grain texture overlaid
for analogue-digital fusion aesthetic.

Composition - Shallow depth of field: sharp subjects, blurred
backgrounds. - Dynamic framing and light motion blur for energy. -
Tension-driven tone: surveillance, pursuit, anticipation.

Advanced Visual Effects - Cinemagraph elements: subtle infinite loops on 
glowing lights and digital interfaces. - Vignette darkening with vignetted 
bloom falloff at edges. - Banding and posterization on color gradients for 
a segmented digital feel. - Chromatic aberration shift animations on hover 
states. - Parallax depth with multiple offset layers suggesting motion 
through digital space. - Volumetric light shafts piercing through haze and 
particle systems. - Radial glow distortion suggesting electromagnetic fields 
around tech elements. - Animated scanlines that drift vertically for a 
CRT/hologram effect. - Pulsing aura effects on key interactive elements 
(buttons, links) with subtle rhythmic breathing. - Glitch transitions using 
displacement mapping and RGB channel separation. - Neon glow overflow with 
soft shadows bleeding beyond element boundaries.

Typography - Clean, geometric, sans-serif fonts (futuristic feel). - 
Subtle text glow with shadow blur for immersion in atmosphere. - All-caps 
or small caps for UI elements reinforcing tech aesthetic. - Thin to regular 
font weights; avoid heavy bold unless for emphasis. - Kerning and letter 
spacing slightly increased for clinical precision.

Interactive States - Hover states intensify chromatic aberration and glow. - 
Click feedback manifests as brief bloom spike or glitch effect. - Focus 
states use bright cyan outline with soft halo. - Disabled states reduce 
saturation and glow intensity dramatically. - Transitions use easing that 
feels mechanical yet smooth (cubic-bezier timing).

Lighting & Shadows - Key light sources create strong directional shadows. - 
Multiple light layers: hard rim light + soft fill light + ambient glow. - 
Shadow color is cool blue/cyan, never neutral black. - Soft shadows with 
high blur radius (8-16px) for volumetric feel. - Inner shadows on recessed 
UI elements suggesting depth and tech layering.

Summary (Short Version) “Futuristic cinematic style with intense bloom,
cold cyan color grading, soft diffusion, atmospheric fog, and a
bleach-bypass look—reflecting the aesthetic of Minority Report.”
